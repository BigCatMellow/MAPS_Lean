# Research Before Architecture

Do not let an unverified assumption become a load-bearing design decision.

For an external, time-sensitive, technical, or domain claim, use this flow:

```text
Question → brief → sources → evaluate → claims/evidence → assumptions → summary → decision or task
```

- Make the question specific and answerable.
- Prefer primary sources: official documentation, source code, standards, or
  direct measurement.
- Record each claim with a precise source location and its retrieval date.
- Mark unsourced beliefs as assumptions, including their blast radius if wrong.
- When sources disagree, preserve the disagreement and either resolve it by
  source quality/recency or keep it open; never average or guess.
- Give time-sensitive conclusions a re-verification date.

Research produces evidence and recommendations. It does not itself authorize
implementation; feed the result into a decision or task.

Start with [the research brief template](../templates/research-brief.md). Use
its claim/source table to keep evidence, assumptions, retrieval dates, and open
questions together until the research can inform a decision or scoped task.
