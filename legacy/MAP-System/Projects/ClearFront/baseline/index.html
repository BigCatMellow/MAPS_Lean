How to play

  • Your hand holds at most 3 cards and refills to 3 when your turn ends.
  • Your Champion sits outside your deck in its own slot. Deploy it for mana whenever you like as one of your 2 plays.
  • A deployed Champion is a unit with an ongoing ability. If destroyed, it returns to its slot at +2 cost.
  • You may play at most 2 cards per turn (deploying your Champion counts as one).
  • You may swap one unwanted card each turn.
  • Click a card in your hand to play it.
  • You have one attack step each turn. You may play cards before or after it.
  • Click ready units to select attackers, then confirm the attack.
  • During defense, click your blocker, then an enemy attacker.
  • The bottom bar shows unblocked damage and projected life.
  • Use Review blocks to see which units survive before resolving combat.
  • Attacks play out as short card clashes. Tap the clash screen to skip.
  • Tap Latest or Log to see why a card was destroyed or discarded.
  • Reduce the opponent from 20 life to 0.

Champions

  • Ember Warden (Emberwild): first damage card each turn deals +1 damage.
  • Verdant Sentinel (Verdant Court): first unit played each turn gains +1 Health.
  • Iron Warden (Iron Covenant): prevents the first 1 damage each turn cycle.
  • Ashen Reaper (Ashen Murder): first friendly death each turn cycle deals 1 damage.
  • Gilded Champion (Gilded Vigil): first damage card each turn deals +2 damage.
  • Thicket Stalker (Thicket Guile): first unit played each turn gains +2 Health; has Rush.

Keywords

  • Charge: Can attack immediately.
  • Rush: Can attack immediately, but cannot hit the opposing player its entry turn.
  • Flying: Only Flying can block it.
  • Shield: Prevent the next damage.
  • Drain: Damage restores that much life.
  • Guard: Must be blocked before other attackers.
  • Stun: Cannot attack during its next turn.

Clearfront rules

  1. Each player starts at 20 life with a 30-card deck made of two factions, plus a Champion kept outside the deck.
  2. Your hand holds at most 3 cards. It refills to 3 at the end of your turn.
  3. Deploy your Champion for mana whenever you like; it counts as one of your 2 plays and enters as a unit.
  4. A destroyed Champion returns to its slot with its cost increased by 2, and can be redeployed later.
  5. While on the battlefield, your Champion grants one ongoing ability, such as +1 damage on your first damage card or +1 Health on your first unit.
  6. The starting player is chosen randomly each game.
  7. Your opening hand always includes at least one 1-cost card.
  8. Once each turn, you may discard one card and immediately draw a replacement.
  9. At the start of your turn, gain 1 maximum mana and refill mana. There is no automatic start-of-turn draw.
  10. Units stay on the board. Spells happen once. Relics give ongoing effects.
  11. You have one attack step each turn and may play cards before or after it.
  12. Units normally wait one turn before attacking. Charge ignores this if you have not used your attack step yet.
  13. The attacker chooses attackers. The defender may assign one blocker to each attacker; each blocker can block only once.
  14. A blocked attacker deals no damage to the player, even when its blocker is destroyed.
  15. Blocked units deal damage to each other at the same time. Unblocked units hit the player.
  16. Damage remains on units until they are healed or destroyed.
  17. Cards can reward damaged enemies, Health gains, Shields, surviving combat, sacrifices, and being played first or second.
  18. Raised, faction-colored cards are Champions. Desaturated cards cannot currently act; their label explains why.
  19. Undo reverses one reversible action. Combat resolution and ending a turn cannot be undone.
  20. Green status labels show when a card’s synergy or bonus is currently active.
