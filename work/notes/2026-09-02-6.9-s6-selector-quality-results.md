Implements path (a) of `work/notes/2026-09-02-6.9-s6-promotion-gate-step.md` §5,
scoped by `work/notes/2026-09-02-6.9-s6-selector-quality-scoping.md`. The frozen
EXP-B corpus (`exp_a_skill_routing_v2.json`, sha256 `2cff0e40…`, **unchanged**)
is the acceptance test.

## What changed

`runtime/context_builder.py::_select_skills` replaces **"any shared token
selects"** with a **match-strength gate**:

- Per-token weight = `1 / (number of catalogued Skills whose name+description
  contains the token)` — a token unique to one Skill = 1.0, shared by three ≈
  0.33. Computed from the candidate catalog alone (`token_skill_count`,
  `Counter` over the catalog token sets); **no retrieval, no synonym map, no
  semantic layer** (those stay gated by roadmap §6.33).
- `_SKILL_ROUTING_STOPWORDS` — a small hand-curated set of process/format words
  (`review`, `plan`, `checklist`, `test`, `service`, …) that score 0 and cannot
  be an anchor.
- A Skill is surfaced iff it has an **anchor** (≥1 matched non-stopword token in
  ≤2 Skills) **and** (`match_strength ≥ 2.0` **or** `match_coverage ≥ 0.5`),
  where coverage = `|matched| / |task signal tokens|`. The coverage arm keeps a
  genuine direct match whose signal words happen to be common ("plan a load
  test for the checkout service" shares only generic words with
  `load-test-planning`, but they are *most* of what the task says).
- The gate sits **after** the SEC4 `capabilities_within_envelope` check (so an
  out-of-envelope declaration is still counted regardless of match strength)
  and **before** the trust gate / body load (a weak match is a silent
  non-selection, exactly as a zero-token match already was).

Floors chosen from intent — "a lone incidental token is not enough; a real
concept-word anchor plus either corroboration or near-total overlap is" — **not
fitted to a corpus number**. The frozen corpora are the regression check.

## Deviation from `#254` §4 acceptance #1 and #3 — and why

The `#254` scoping note listed three gaps to close: HARD_NEGATIVE, AMBIGUOUS,
and VOCABULARY_SHIFT's V01. **Only HARD_NEGATIVE is closed.** A full scoring
simulation against the frozen corpus (run before implementation, confirmed by
@soda) showed the other two are **§6.33-class (semantic), not lexically
closeable**:

### V01 (VOCABULARY_SHIFT morphology) — not closeable

`#254` proposed a plural/tense lemmatiser so `credential` (task) matches
`credentials` (Skill). It does — but V01's resulting match is then **one
distinctive token** (`credential`, weight 1.0), which is **lexically identical**
to hard-negatives H02 (`rotation` → `secrets-rotation`, weight 1.0) and H03
(`api` → `api-contract-review`, weight 1.0). Any strength floor that rejects
H02/H03 rejects V01; any floor that admits V01 re-admits H02/H03. There is no
lexical discriminator — the difference ("is the task *about* credentials, or
does it just contain the word") is exactly what §6.33 semantic retrieval is
gated to provide.

**Decision: do not add the lemmatiser.** It would be unused normalisation
(scope creep) that changes no corpus outcome. V01 joins V02–V04 as a documented
`missed_activation` — now for a *principled* reason (below the relevance floor),
not synonym-blind-by-accident.

### AMBIGUOUS — not closeable without regressing MULTI_SKILL

`#254` proposed a margin rule: emit `AMBIGUOUS` when ≥2 candidates score within
a margin. But A01/A02 are **not score-ties** — the token evidence favours one
candidate (A01 → `dependency-upgrade-review` at 2.5 vs 0.33/0.33; A02 →
nothing clears the gate). A margin loose enough to fire on them would fire on
**M01, which is a genuine exact tie** (`incident-response-playbook` 3.0 ==
`secrets-rotation` 3.0, and both genuinely apply) — regressing MULTI_SKILL from
1.00. MULTI_SKILL ("both apply") and AMBIGUOUS ("can't tell which") are the same
lexical signal; separating them needs the semantic capability §6.33 gates.

**Decision: do not add the margin rule.** A01/A02 stay `ambiguity_misses`.

## Net effect on the 6.9/S6 DONE story

`#254` §6 already routed the residual VOCABULARY_SHIFT gap to a **path (b)
§17.3 reviewer/operator ruling**. After this PR, that ruling covers **both**
residual gaps with one decision:

> *Is a Skill router that is perfect on DIRECT / PARAPHRASE / MULTI_SKILL /
> NO_SKILL and produces zero false activations, but is blind to pure synonym
> shifts and cannot label genuine ambiguity, acceptable to promote 6.9/S6 to
> DONE for a P2 routing capability with the 6.22 trust gate downstream — or
> does the residual motivate the §6.33 semantic work first?*

This PR's recommendation to that ruling (not made here): **acceptable**. The
safety-relevant failure (false activation — a wrong Skill surfaced as evidence)
is eliminated; a synonym-blind *missed* activation costs recall, not safety; and
ambiguity surfaces the single best lexical candidate rather than nothing.

**No checklist STATUS flip in this PR** — the flip is the separate reviewer
gate step (re-run `work/notes/2026-09-02-6.9-s6-promotion-gate-step.md`'s gate
against these numbers).
